THE PRACTICAL PLAYBOOK

Do less harm indoors.

01 / REMOVE

Reduce the source.

Avoid indoor burning, including incense and smoking. Use kitchen extraction while cooking when possible.

02 / FILTER

Choose fit, not hype.

Use a suitable particle filter and check smoke CADR against room size. Maintain and replace filters as instructed.

03 / BALANCE

Ventilate thoughtfully.

Ventilation needs depend on indoor sources and outdoor conditions. Don't assume windows must always remain closed.
